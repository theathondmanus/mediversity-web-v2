We are delighted to announce a new strategic partnership with **Renji Hospital, Affiliated to Shanghai Jiao Tong University School of Medicine** — one of China's most prestigious, long‑established and top‑Tier A comprehensive hospitals.

This official collaboration brings together Renji Hospital's renowned medical expertise, advanced clinical technology, and integrated healthcare system with our global service capabilities, to launch **customized health check‑up and medical solutions** designed exclusively for international clients.

## What Clients Will Gain Access To

- Personalized health screening and medical consultation plans
- Direct care from Renji's world‑class specialist teams
- Streamlined, international patient‑centered service processes
- Comprehensive, high‑quality cross‑border healthcare support

## Why This Partnership Matters

By integrating premium domestic medical resources with international service standards, we aim to build a more accessible, reliable and professional healthcare bridge for global patients seeking medical services in China.

This partnership represents an important milestone in advancing **cross‑border healthcare innovation** and enhancing the international medical experience.

We welcome individuals, corporate clients and institutional partners worldwide to connect with us and explore tailored healthcare solutions and long‑term cooperation opportunities.
