On December 13, 2025, the International Medical Service (IMS) of Peking Union Medical College Hospital (PUMCH) hosted the "Approach PUMCH – Open Day & Experience Day" event. More than 30 representatives from embassies and consulates of various countries in China, commercial health insurance institutions, and well-known enterprises and public institutions gathered together, with representatives from Mediversity Global's Beijing Office also invited to attend.

Through on-site visits, interactive experiences, and popular science lectures, the Mediversity team gained an in-depth understanding of PUMCH's profound heritage and comprehensive strength in medical inheritance, integration of traditional Chinese and Western medicine, health management, and international services.

## Recognising PUMCH's Position

As a benchmark in China's medical field, Peking Union Medical College Hospital is renowned for its profound hospital history, rigorous medical system, and cutting-edge diagnosis and treatment capabilities. During the Open Day, we delved into PUMCH's historical context, experienced its characteristic medical services and advanced equipment, and deeply felt the top medical institution's ultimate practice of "patient-centered care" — which is highly consistent with Mediversity's long-standing philosophy of "high-quality medical care and professional services."

## Looking Forward

This invitation not only reflects PUMCH's recognition of Mediversity's professional strength in international healthcare collaboration but also marks another important step in Mediversity's continuous expansion of its global high-quality medical cooperation network. Moving forward, Mediversity will continue to strengthen exchanges and cooperation with top medical institutions like Peking Union Medical College Hospital, committed to bridging high-quality medical resources and providing more professional and reliable international healthcare services for global clients.
