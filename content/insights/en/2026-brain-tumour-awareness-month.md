This March, we stand with the UK and global communities to raise awareness for brain tumours—a condition that impacts millions of lives.

## Key Facts & Data

- Brain tumours are the leading cancer killer of children and young adults under 40 in the UK.
- Around 13,000 people are diagnosed with a primary brain tumour each year in the UK.
- The 5-year survival rate for malignant brain cancer in England is just 12.9%, far below the average for all cancers (56%).
- Globally, late-stage and complex brain tumours remain one of the most challenging conditions to treat.
