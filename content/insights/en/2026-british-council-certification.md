Mediversity Global is pleased to announce a key professional milestone: on December 4, 2025, the company has been officially certified by the British Council through its prestigious UK Agent and Counsellor Training programme.

This certification validates our team's professional capabilities and reinforces our commitment to high-standard, reliable services in medical English education and international healthcare collaboration.

## About the British Council

To highlight the significance of this achievement, it is crucial to emphasize the authority of the British Council — the UK's official international organization for cultural relations and education, founded in 1934. As a UK public body and registered charity governed by a Royal Charter, it operates in over 200 countries, building cross-border connections and upholding the highest standards of integrity. Its certifications are highly respected globally in education and international services.

## What This Means for Our Clients

For Mediversity Global, this certification is a testament to our commitment to professional, high-quality medical English education guidance. It validates our team's expertise and ethical practices, strengthening trust with healthcare professionals, medical learners and global partners. We will leverage this certification to enhance our services and better serve global clients.

Institutions and organizations seeking to enhance medical English training or explore international medical education and tourism partnerships are welcome to connect with our certified team for tailored solutions.
