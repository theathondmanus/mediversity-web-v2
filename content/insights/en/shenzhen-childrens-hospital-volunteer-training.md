## Project overview

In October 2026, Mediversity Global (MVG) delivered a healthcare translation volunteer training programme for Shenzhen Children's Hospital. Working to a demanding schedule, MVG completed the full preparation process — from initial engagement, needs analysis and curriculum development to proposal refinement, educator coordination, teaching preparation and delivery — in approximately two weeks.

## Course design

The programme was designed around the communication challenges of paediatric healthcare, combining the hospital's objectives, the doctors' professional backgrounds and their day-to-day practice. It brought together four connected learning components: consultation opening and communication, accurate medical interpreting, cross-cultural clinical communication and integrated clinical simulation.
