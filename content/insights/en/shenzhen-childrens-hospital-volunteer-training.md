## Project overview

In October 2026, Shenzhen Children's Hospital organised targeted training for healthcare translation volunteers, bringing together 22 doctors from 17 hospital departments and units. Mediversity Global (MVG) designed a course in clinical communication and medical interpreting around the doctors' professional backgrounds and paediatric consultations.

The intention to proceed was confirmed on 27 October, and the training took place three days later, on 30 October.

## Course design

The course was organised around the consultation opening, information transfer and communication with families, through four connected components: starting the consultation, accurate medical interpreting, cross-cultural communication and integrated role-play, all practised within a simulated paediatric consultation.
